**First Presentation: Anne Bertucio de Hollembeak (Roost) and Alex Garnett (Bluesky) present Coop and Jetstream**

Roost and Bluesky will be co-presenting on open moderation tools for open social platforms. Roost recently released Coop: An open source review and moderation platform for anything that requires decisions at scale. Coop is the first free, end-to-end CSAM detection, review, and NCMEC reporting workflow. Bluesky recently released a new version of their open Jetstream API. Together, Coop and Jetstream make it easier than ever to run your own trust and safety architecture, providing big-world scale to communities of all kinds.

**Second Presentation: Navigator: open-source cloud-native services for justice**

Navigator is an open-source platform for running a law firm. It brings together intake, matter management, project repositories, communications, and publishing. Its AI agent harness gives agents tools and rules for working across those systems.

We’ll run a fictional Oregon probate workflow live, then follow it through PostgreSQL and OpenTelemetry. I’ll show how lessons from building and operating software become instructions, tools, and checks, and how you can point your own agents at the repository to borrow what fits.
